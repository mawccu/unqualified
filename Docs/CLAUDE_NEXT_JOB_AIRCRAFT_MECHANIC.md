# Proposed next job: Aircraft Maintenance Technician

The user wants a job substantially different from wedding work. This is a proposed direction for the next slice, not authorization to replace the current job or a claim that this content exists.

## The pitch

Four people who have never opened a toolbox are hired to service a small aircraft. They must discover faults, use the right equipment, and convince a supervisor that they know what they are doing. Keep it an exaggerated fictional maintenance puzzle, not an authentic aviation training simulator.

The main verbs are inspect, diagnose, dismantle, repair, reassemble, and test. Avoid reskinning food delivery, seating, or cake stacking. Start with one grounded fictional aircraft in a compact hangar, a tool bench, parts shelves, and a test console. No flying simulation is required for this job.

## Three connected challenges

1. Landing gear: stabilize the aircraft using marked equipment, remove a wheel with matching tools, identify a damaged component, replace it, and secure the assembly. Provide visible fit and tightening feedback rather than a random button sequence.
2. Electrical fault: follow an accessible fictional schematic, use a tester to narrow down a fault, isolate the test circuit, replace the faulty module, and verify the indicator sequence. Change fault locations between runs while keeping every configuration solvable.
3. Ground test: one player reads the test checklist while another operates controls and a third watches the external gauges. Solo mode offers a clipboard that can be pinned beside the console; no simultaneous distant actions are mandatory.

Hands grip tools, turn fasteners, support removed parts, and flinch at a harmless test backfire. Mistakes produce specific symptoms that can be diagnosed and corrected. Essential components remain recoverable.

## CV lies and pretending

- “Landing gear specialist”: receives the more involved mechanical fault and a quality bonus opportunity.
- “Electrical diagnostics expert”: must identify the faulty module rather than simply replace a marked one.
- “Certified inspection lead”: signs the checklist and explains a test result to the supervisor using evidence gathered during repair.

Show consequences before applying. A believable bluff can buy inspection time, but cannot turn a failed test into a repair. Keep the supervisor's evidence and suspicion separate from the aircraft's actual condition.

## Optional play

Movable tool trolley, rolling mechanic's stool, working inspection lamp, air hose with bounded comic effects, opening storage drawers, loose nuts and washers, and a radio. Reuse existing interaction and collision systems. Do not let the stool or trolley launch players or permanently trap critical parts.

## Scope and completion

First build one full fault → diagnosis → repair → test → inspection → payout loop. Then add a small pool of fault variants. Preserve the apartment/CV return loop and existing wedding job. Add this listing as playable only after its scene and progression work. Demonstrate both solo completion and co-op where networking is actually implemented.
