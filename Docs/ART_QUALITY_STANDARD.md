# Art quality standard

Taken from the strongest assets we have: the workshop props, the office set, the new beds and the window. The tone is stylised and slightly shabby comedy. People live and work in these places on no money.

**Shape.** Chunky, readable silhouettes at 2 to 4 m. Soft bevels (2 to 12 mm, two segments) on every hard edge, so edges catch light instead of aliasing. No knife-edge boxes.

**Scale.** Real-world sizes:
- a worktop at 0.9 m,
- a seat at 0.45 m,
- a mattress 0.18 m thick,
- a door 2.1 m.

Small interactive parts may be shown up to 1.5x when they must be aimed at (the workshop parts); say so in code.

**Surfaces and materials.**
- Flat colours named after their surface ("Old pine", "Bed sheet"), shared through `MaterialLibrary`. One material per real surface. A model that needs a variant gets a per-model override, never a change to a shared material.
- Fabric, paper and wood are matte (smoothness 0.1 to 0.3).
- Only metal, glass and wet surfaces shine.
- Nothing soft is metallic or mirror-glossy.
- Texture sets for large areas (floor, walls, fabric): about 1 UV unit per metre, tiling 0.5 to 5.
- Small props are flat colour.

**Shading.** Rounded and soft things (bedding, cushions, food) are smooth-shaded. Machined things are flat or smoothed by angle.

**Contact.** Everything rests on something:
- no gaps over 1 cm,
- no props inside furniture,
- colliders follow what you can put things on (`ColliderFit`).

`Tools/Eval/props_support.cs` and `PolishTests` check this.

**Lighting.**
- Baked for the rooms; light probes for anything that moves.
- A moving object near a strong light uses a probe anchor away from the beam.
- Emissive surfaces (windows, screens) glow only enough to read as lit. They are not light panels.
- Avoid wall mottling: at least 36 texels per metre in small rooms, and a denoiser.
- Post-processing: ACES, mild bloom (threshold 1). Bloom and darkness don't cover problems.

**Set dressing.** It explains the place (a pallet bed, a parcel mat, a pegboard) and leads the eye to what you can use. Repeated clutter is varied in rotation and position. Never scatter props to fill space.

**UI.**
- Reference resolution 1600x900, matched between width and height.
- Body text 14 to 16 px; secondary text 12 to 13 px (at least 10 px on a 720p screen).
- Titles 20 to 26 px bold.
- One primary (orange) action per card.
- Colour is never the only signal: ticks, labels and words go with it.
- Long explanations are opened on request (the Tab card, the claim just ticked, tooltips), not shown at once.
- While a full-screen interface is open, the HUD steps aside.

**Provenance.** Every new asset is generated by a script in `Tools/` (Blender or Python) and recorded in `Docs/ASSET_REGISTER.md`.
