# 09 — Backlog, names, and open decisions

Everything here is optional future exploration unless promoted into a milestone. A creative backlog is not an implementation to-do list.

## Naming candidates — unverified

UNQUALIFIED; References Available; Trust Us, We're Professionals; Questionable Qualifications; Hired Somehow; Professionally Unprepared; Probably Certified.

“UNQUALIFIED” is the internal working title only. Short words are easy to remember but may be crowded. Search Steam, broader game platforms, relevant trademark records and domains before final branding. No commercial availability or legal clearance is asserted for any candidate.

## Additional résumé claim ideas

| Claim | Possible consequence | Why not in prototype |
|---|---|---|
| I work alone | Teammates must appear to be visitors during inspection | Risk of sidelining players |
| Fluent in every technical term | Match an authored icon glossary during a demo | Needs localization/accessibility design |
| No replacements necessary | Spare substitution is a witnessed violation | Good later reuse |
| Always camera-ready | Client requests a visible presentation checkpoint | Requires camera/framing logic |
| I personally inspect everything | Selected player signs three completed checkpoints | Could become busywork |
| We finish early | Known shorter clock | M3 fast-turnaround variant |
| We keep clients informed | One progress briefing at a known time | Must avoid stopping gameplay |
| We use only premium materials | Approved cheap substitute loses claim bonus if seen | Strong quality/appearance tradeoff |

Select claims that change decisions, not merely increase speed or reduce health. Each needs a clear non-microphone path and a viable solo solution.

## Career flavor without expensive systems

- A résumé that accumulates absurd but factual debrief statistics.
- Apartment objects unlocked from job stories: damaged cake trophy, foam-covered portrait, spare aircraft seat.
- Agency messages reacting to actual previous shift incidents.
- Employee awards: “Most Convincing Nodding,” “Recovered the Unrecoverable,” “Read the Manual Professionally.”
- A final week offer with two compatible claims and one unusual client condition.
- Daily seed challenges only after deterministic content generation works; no leaderboard/backend requirement initially.

## Ideas explicitly parked

Live AI recruiters; full procedural maps; open-world city; full vehicle driving/flying; realistic surgery; combat; hostile-player roles; six-plus players; split screen; dedicated servers; host migration; mod support; automatic video replay; user-generated missions; console ports; mandatory voice; battle pass; marketplace economy.

These may be entertaining, but they would substantially alter cost, moderation, testing or architecture. Do not add them because a code model says they are “easy.”

## Content authoring templates

### Event

ID; eligible jobs; preconditions; warning text/cue and duration; affected entities; authoritative state changes; incident IDs created; player recovery; solo override; cooldown; incompatible events; maximum duration; reset behavior; test seed.

### Claim

ID; exact promise text; eligible jobs; bonus; tradeoff; detectable trigger; one-time/repeat behavior; evidence produced; recovery; solo validity; incompatible claims; localization keys; test cases.

### Job

Pitch; map footprint; three essential outcomes; completion and quality formulas; tasks/dependencies; objects/tools; witness route; inspection checkpoints; three claim candidates; event pool; recovery routes; player-count quantities; new technical cost; content budget; likely trailer moment; acceptance checklist.

## Questions the owner can answer later

How much weekly development time is realistic? Is there an existing Unity/Godot preference? Who will own art/audio? Is a dark-humor surgery job desirable at all? What budget is available for assets and testing? Is the intended commercial scope a small complete game or an expandable project?

None blocks M0–M2. The current defaults are sufficient to start a prototype without pretending the entire business is already settled.
